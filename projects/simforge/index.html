SimForge

  • Jan 2025
  • •Tags:

Framework for diverse virtual environments through procedural generation of simulation assets.

SimForge is a framework for creating diverse virtual environments through procedural generation. Instead of hand-modeling every rock, terrain patch or spacecraft, you describe an asset once and generate as many variants as you need, each selected by a random seed. SimForge produces the procedural assets of Space Robotics Bench, where they let every simulated scene differ from the last.

Live demo

Choose an asset and a seed, then open the 3D viewer. Each model is a GLB file that SimForge exported for that seed, and you can download it.

Features

  • Assets: registered building blocks, from images and meshes to articulated models. Their definitions live in external repositories that projects can share, such as SimForge Foundry.
  • Generators: create assets from those definitions, with a seed selecting each variant. The Blender generator builds geometry and materials and exports them to formats such as GLB, USD and OBJ.
  • Integrations: bring generated assets into game engines and physics simulators. The Isaac Lab integration spawns them in NVIDIA Isaac Lab.
  • Command line: simforge ls lists the registered assets, and simforge gen moon_rock -e glb -s 0 -n 3 generates three variants.

Status

The latest release is SimForge 0.4.0 (May 2026) on PyPI. It is dual-licensed under MIT or Apache 2.0, and the SimForge Foundry asset definitions under CC0 1.0.
