About

CV

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Space Robotics at the University of Luxembourg. I also did my PhD there, with the thesis Robot Learning Beyond Earth: Enabling Adaptive Autonomy in Space. I work on robot learning, reinforcement learning and simulation, so that robots can learn to act in the most extreme environments.

During my PhD, I was a visiting researcher at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, working on scalable simulation and sim-to-real transfer for space robotics. Before my PhD, I earned a BSc and an MSc in Robotics at Aalborg University. I also worked as a Rust software engineer at Aivero, which started my passion for Rust and systems programming.
