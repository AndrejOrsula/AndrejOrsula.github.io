Andrej Orsula

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Luxembourg, working on space robotics and artificial intelligence. I develop robots that learn to adapt to a changing world, on Earth and in space. I also build open-source tools for robot learning and simulation, with a soft spot for Rust.
