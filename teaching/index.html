Robotic Manipulation in Space

🗓️ 2022 – Present📍 University of Luxembourg🏛️ Master in Space Technologies and Business

I have been preparing and teaching the practical aspects of Robotic Manipulation in Space to third-semester students of the Master in Space Technologies and Business (formerly the Interdisciplinary Space Master) at the University of Luxembourg. The curriculum emphasizes hands-on experience with real robotic hardware to build a comprehensive understanding of the core principles behind robotic manipulation in the context of space.

🦾 Robot Kinematics
🗺️ Motion Planning
🎮 Robot Control
🔭 Space Applications
